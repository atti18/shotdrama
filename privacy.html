Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shot Drama (the "Service"), operated by OPERATOR_NAME, handles information when you connect your TikTok account.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok basic profile (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to post content you approve.
  • Creator posting info returned by TikTok (available privacy options, interaction settings, posting limits), used to render the posting screen.
  • Content you choose to post: video files, titles and captions.

We do not collect your TikTok password, contacts, messages, or browsing history.

2. How we use information

  • To upload videos to your TikTok drafts (video.upload) or publish them to your profile (video.publish) when you confirm a post.
  • To display the status of your posts.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for advertising. Information is sent only to TikTok as needed to perform the actions you request.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and queued content are stored on a private server controlled by the operator and are not publicly accessible. Tokens are used only for the Service and are protected with access controls.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect or revoke access. Uploaded video files are deleted from our server after they are successfully posted, or within 30 days. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), and you may request deletion of all stored data by emailing us; we will delete it within 30 days.

5. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
