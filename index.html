Original short-form dramas, published to TikTok.

Shot Drama is a small creator studio tool. We produce our own original short drama episodes and use Shot Drama to review, schedule and publish them to our TikTok account.

Our channel: @thejoseonoracle — original Joseon-era fortune-teller short dramas.

How it works

1. Connect

The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the account.

2. Review

Finished episodes appear in a queue with a preview, title and caption.

3. Choose settings

The creator picks privacy level, comment / duet / stitch options and content disclosure.

4. Post

Nothing is posted until the creator clicks "Post to TikTok" or sends it to drafts.
